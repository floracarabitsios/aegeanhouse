# AegeanHouse
The Aegean House prototype merges the tennents of house music with Greek Island music specifically from the Cycladic Islands to create a musical masterpiece. 

To view the project on EarSketch follow this link: https://earsketch.gatech.edu/earsketch2/?sharing=NTF91_vYE9Xe7I4-RRDSsw

## General Information
- The song incorperates aspects of both House Music and Greek Island music to take listeners back to a Grecian Holiday.
- Total playing time: 1 minute and 57 seconds.
- Inspired by House music artists such as Keinemusik and Greek Island music artists such as Giannis Parios. 


## Technologies Used
- EarSketch
- iMovie


## Features
- Incoorperates both aspects of desired genres.
- Uses tape recordings from my Great Grandfather singing songs from the island Paros.
- Emphasises sounds such as church bells and cicadas which are central to Greek living.
